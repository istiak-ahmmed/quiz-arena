Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuizArena ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our platform at quizarena.com.bd.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following when you register and use QuizArena:

  • Account data: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and referral code (if used).
  • Payment data: MFS phone numbers and transaction IDs you submit for deposits. We do not store full MFS account credentials.
  • Quiz performance data: Answers submitted, time per question (server-side), final scores, and rankings.
  • Anti-cheat signals: Tab visibility changes (document.hidden events) logged during active quiz sessions.
  • Device/technical data: IP address, browser type, and session tokens.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account, verify deposits, and process withdrawals.
  • To calculate quiz scores, rankings, and distribute prizes fairly.
  • To enforce anti-cheat policies and resolve disputes.
  • To credit referral bonuses when the conditions are met.
  • To communicate important account and event updates by email/SMS.

3. Payment & Financial Data

We do not store any MFS PIN numbers, account passwords, or full card numbers. Deposits are processed by an admin verifying the TxID and amount you submit. Withdrawals are sent to the MFS number stored in your profile. We maintain a full ledger of all transactions for dispute resolution.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your name and rank are displayed publicly on event leaderboards. Phone numbers are never displayed publicly. We may share data with law enforcement if legally required.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. Transaction ledgers are retained indefinitely for dispute resolution and regulatory purposes. You may request account deletion by emailing support@quizarena.com.bd — wallet balance must be ৳0 before deletion.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this policy: support@quizarena.com.bd
