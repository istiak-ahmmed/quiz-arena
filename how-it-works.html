How QuizArena Works

Everything you need to know about competing, winning, and cashing out — in plain language.

1 Getting Started

A
Create a Free Account

Register with your name, email, and phone number. No credit card required. Your phone number is used for MFS payment matching.

B
Recharge Your Wallet

Send money via bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or Upay to our collection number. Submit the TxID + amount. Admin verifies and credits your wallet within minutes.

C
Browse & Enroll in Events

Browse upcoming quiz events by category. Pay the entry fee from your wallet to enroll. Registration closes at the event deadline.

2 Playing the Quiz

The Quiz Runner Interface

  • →Full-screen, distraction-free layout. Navigation is hidden during play.
  • →One question at a time. Four large answer buttons for easy tapping on mobile.
  • →The countdown ring at the top changes color — Indigo → Gold → Red — as time runs low.
  • →No correct/incorrect feedback shown mid-quiz. All results are revealed after the event closes.

Anti-Cheat System

  • ⚠Question/option order is uniquely shuffled per participant. Copying from others won't help.
  • ⚠Timing is 100% server-side. The client clock is only decorative.
  • ⚠Tab switching is logged. Exceed the limit (usually 2) and your session is flagged.
  • ⚠Browser blur/focus events are recorded but treated as signals, not automatic disqualifications.

3 Ranking & Prize Distribution

How Ranking Works

Primary sort: score (correct answers). Tiebreaker: total time taken — server-derived by summing (answer submission time − question served time) for every question. Faster total time at the same score = higher rank.

How Prize Pool is Built

Prize pool = total entry fees × prize pool percentage (e.g. 70%). Platform keeps the rest as operating revenue. The prize pool figure shown on event pages is a live estimate based on current enrollment.

Prize Split

Example split: 1st = 50%, 2nd = 30%, 3rd = 20% of the prize pool. Prize amounts are fixed at event-close time based on final enrollment count and credited to winner wallets automatically.

4 Withdrawing Your Winnings

Request Withdrawal

Go to Wallet → Withdraw. Enter the amount (min ৳100) and your MFS number. Request submitted for admin processing.

Admin Processes

Admin reviews and sends payment to your MFS number. Processed within 24 hours on business days. No platform fees.

Money in Your Account

Withdrawal marked as completed. Funds arrive in your bKash/Nagad/Rocket/Upay account and your wallet ledger is updated.
