FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The prize pool is dynamically built from entry fees. For example, if an event has a 70% prize pool percentage and 100 participants each paid ৳50, the total collected is ৳5,000 — and ৳3,500 goes to winners. The remaining 30% is the platform fee. Winners split the pool: 1st gets 50%, 2nd gets 30%, 3rd gets 20% of the prize pool.
Deposits are typically verified within 5–30 minutes during business hours. An admin reviews your transaction ID against our MFS records. Once approved, your wallet is credited instantly. You'll receive a notification when it's done.
Tab switches are logged. Each event has a maximum allowed tab-switch count (usually 2). If you exceed it, your session is flagged and may be auto-submitted or disqualified. The quiz detects browser focus loss — this is to maintain fair competition.
If two participants have the same score, the one who answered in less total time ranks higher. Time is measured server-side from when each question was served to when you submitted your answer — so answering quickly matters, but it can't be gamed client-side.
There is a minimum withdrawal threshold (currently ৳100). Withdrawal requests are processed manually and sent to your registered MFS number within 24 hours on business days. There are no withdrawal fees charged by the platform.
If an event is cancelled — either by admin or because the minimum participant count wasn't reached by the registration deadline — all entry fees are fully refunded to participant wallets automatically, with no action required on your part.
