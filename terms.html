Terms of Service

Last updated: July 25, 2026

By creating an account or participating in any QuizArena event, you agree to these Terms. Please read them carefully.

1. Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 years old, or 16+ with verifiable parental consent.
  • One account per person. Multiple accounts are prohibited and result in permanent ban.
  • You must reside in Bangladesh. MFS payments are Bangladesh-only.

2. Wallet & Payments

  • Wallet balance is only usable on QuizArena. It is not transferable to other users.
  • Deposits are credited after manual admin verification. Fraudulent TxIDs result in account ban.
  • Withdrawals are processed to the MFS number registered on your profile within 24 hours on business days.
  • QuizArena is not responsible for MFS transfer failures caused by incorrect numbers provided by the user.

3. Event Participation & Anti-Cheat

  • Entry fees are deducted from your wallet upon enrollment. Non-refundable unless the event is cancelled by admin.
  • Events with fewer than the minimum number of participants are cancelled and entry fees fully refunded.
  • Tab switching is monitored. Exceeding the allowed limit (default: 2) results in auto-submit. Your submitted answers are scored as-is.
  • Using tools, scripts, or assistance to gain an unfair advantage constitutes cheating and results in permanent disqualification and ban.
  • Timing is 100% server-side. Clients cannot manipulate time spent per question.
  • Admin decisions on fraud and cheating are final.

4. Prizes & Ranking

  • Prizes are calculated as a percentage of total entry fees collected after the event closes.
  • Tiebreaker: total time taken server-side. Faster time at equal score = higher rank.
  • Prize amounts are credited to your wallet automatically after event results are published. No manual claim needed.
  • Platform retains (100% − Prize Pool %) as operating revenue. This percentage is published on every event page.

5. Referral Program

  • Referral bonuses are credited when the referred person completes their first paid quiz event.
  • Self-referrals (referring yourself via a second account) are prohibited and result in ban.
  • QuizArena reserves the right to modify or terminate the referral program at any time.

6. Account Suspension & Termination

QuizArena may suspend or terminate accounts without prior notice for: fraudulent deposits, cheating, multiple accounts, harassment, or any violation of these Terms. Wallet balance in banned accounts is forfeited if fraud is confirmed.

7. Limitation of Liability

QuizArena is a skill-based competitive platform. Outcomes depend on knowledge and speed. We do not guarantee any particular financial result. Total liability is limited to the amount in your wallet balance at the time of any dispute.

8. Changes to Terms

We may update these Terms. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance. Contact support@quizarena.com.bd for questions.
