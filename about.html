About QuizArena

Built for students and knowledge enthusiasts across Bangladesh who want to compete, learn, and earn.

Our Mission

A fair arena for intellectual competition

QuizArena was built with a simple belief: knowledge should be rewarded. Traditional quiz competitions are limited by geography, entry barriers, and subjective judging. We remove all of that — every question is objective, every timer is fair, every payout is transparent.

The platform handles real money, so we've built it with that responsibility in mind — manual payment verification, immutable transaction ledgers, server-side timing that can't be gamed, and auto-refund policies when events don't meet minimum thresholds.

What We Stand For

Fairness

Server-side timing, shuffled questions, and transparent ranking formulas. No edge from client-side tricks.

Transparency

Every transaction is logged. Prize calculations are public. Refund policies are explicit. No hidden fees.

Accessibility

Mobile-first design. bKash/Nagad/Rocket/Upay support. Entry fees from ৳20 — competitive quiz isn't just for the privileged.

Knowledge-First

Questions cover real subjects — history, science, literature, current affairs. This is a genuine knowledge competition.
